## 4. Known Risks & Untested Assumptions
- Remote OTA updates over HTTP `/flash.json` require the unit to reboot into recovery partition (`/recovery.json` or `/reboot_ota.json`) before the flashing endpoint becomes active.
- Flashing over Wi-Fi carries risk of bricking if recovery partition is corrupted; physical USB-serial flashing via `/dev/ttyUSB0` (aliased to `/dev/ttyUSB-esp32`) remains the safest method for initial bare-metal firmware loads.
